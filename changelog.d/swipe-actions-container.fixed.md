- Apple: **One revealed swipe action at a time.** Revealing a swipe action
  on a message row now retracts any other row's, and tapping elsewhere
  retracts the open one; revealed actions no longer pile up or survive a
  trip through the reader. The rows host their swipe actions in one shared
  container instead of a private list each, which also lets the leading
  swipe win on a tiled iPad split. The apps now require iOS, iPadOS, macOS,
  and visionOS 27.
