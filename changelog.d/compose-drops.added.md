- Apple: **Drop files into a message.** Files, images and messages
  dropped onto the compose form above the message body (the From,
  recipient and subject rows, or an attachment) become attachments, on
  iPhone, iPad, iPhone Duo and the Mac: from Files, Photos or another
  app beside Cabalmail in Split View, from the Finder, or as an .eml
  from a Cabalmail message list in another window. Text dropped onto a
  field still lands in the field.
