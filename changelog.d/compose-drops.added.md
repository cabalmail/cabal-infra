- Apple: **Drop files into a message.** On iPhone, iPad and iPhone
  Duo, files, images and messages dropped onto the compose form above
  the message body (the From, recipient and subject rows, or an
  attachment) become attachments: from Files, Photos or another app
  beside Cabalmail in Split View, or as an .eml from a Cabalmail message
  list in another window. Send waits until a dropped item has been read.
  Text dropped onto a recipient or subject field still lands in the
  field.
