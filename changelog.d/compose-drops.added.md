- Apple: **Drop files into a message.** On iPhone, iPad and iPhone
  Duo, files, images and messages dropped onto a message being
  written become attachments: onto the From, recipient or subject row,
  an attachment, or the message body in Rich Text or Markdown. They can
  come from Files, Photos or another app beside Cabalmail in Split View,
  or, as an .eml, from a Cabalmail message list in another window. Send
  waits until a dropped item has been read. Text dropped onto a
  recipient or subject field still lands in the field, and text dropped
  onto the body is still inserted.
