- Apple: **Search covers feeds as well as mail.** Search has an All,
  Mail and Feeds control at the top of its results, on every layout. A
  search opens on the medium in front: Mail from a folder, Feeds from a
  feed list, and All from anywhere else, the Flagged view included. All
  shows a section for each medium with its count and its first five
  matches, and a Show all row that moves the control to that medium;
  Mail is the results list as it was, and Feeds lists every matching
  article, newest first. Feed results come from the articles downloaded
  to the device, in every subscribed feed, and the list says so. Unread,
  the flag, Since and Before narrow both; with From, To, Subject, Has
  attachment or This folder only set, feeds are not searched and the
  Feeds section says why. A result opens in its own reader, and an open
  article survives a change of layout as an open message does.
