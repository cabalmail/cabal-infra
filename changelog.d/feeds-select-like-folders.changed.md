- Apple: **Feeds select like folders.** In the Mac's sidebar a feed, a
  feed folder and All Feeds are now rows of the list's own selection,
  beside the mail folders, where they were buttons drawn inside it. A
  selected feed takes the sidebar's highlight as a selected folder does,
  and the Up and Down arrow keys move through the folders and on into the
  feeds. Nothing changes on iPhone, iPad or Apple Vision Pro: the iPad's
  sidebar keeps its feed rows as they were, and the others give Feeds a
  sidebar of its own. Under it, on every platform, the folder list's
  selection is one `SidebarSelection` (a mail folder by its path, a feed
  list, or a view of both media, which no sidebar offers yet), a window's
  stored route can name such a view and still reads the routes older
  builds stored, and CabalmailKit has `ItemRef`, one identity for a
  message or a feed item.
