- Apple: **A message read in the Flagged view no longer reopens in its
  folder.** A message opened from the Flagged view on a closed iPhone Duo,
  or in a narrowed iPad window, and carried across the Duo opening or the
  window widening, was also left waiting for its own folder's list. It
  opened there once the view was left: after a pick of that folder in the
  sidebar, or after going back to the folder list and opening the Duo
  again. A window restored on the view with a message open had the same
  message waiting beneath it. The folder a window lands on beneath the
  view now has none of the view's messages scheduled for it (#2577).
