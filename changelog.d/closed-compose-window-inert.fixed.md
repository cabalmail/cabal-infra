- Apple: **A closed compose window can't come back with its draft.**
  On iPad and iPhone Duo the system keeps listing a compose window in
  its window switcher after Send or Discard closes it, and picking it
  there brought back a live composer holding the sent or discarded
  draft, Send included (#2257). Once a closed window has left the
  screen it holds nothing, and one picked from the switcher steps
  straight back out to the mail window. The switcher still lists it
  until it is swiped away: on a phone, iOS refuses an app's request to
  close a window for good.
