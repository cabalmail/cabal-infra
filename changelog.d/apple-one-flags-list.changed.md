- Apple: **One flags list.** The built-in flag is now an entry in
  Settings > Flags: first in the list, and named "Flagged" until you
  rename it. It can be renamed, recolored and moved like a flag you
  added; it cannot be switched off, and where those offer Delete it
  offers Reset to Default. A flagged message shows a dot in that entry's
  color on its row and a named chip in the reader, in the same order as
  the list, in place of the separate flag glyph. A flagged feed item
  shows the same dot beside its date instead of a large flag at the
  row's edge. The first enabled flag in the list is the default flag,
  marked Default once there is a second one: the flag swipe, the Flag
  button in the reader and the bulk bar, the first item of the message
  menus and the flag shortcuts (Shift-Command-8 in the list,
  Shift-Command-L in the reader) set and clear it, and take its name
  and color once it is anything but the built-in flag under its
  original name. Make Default in a flag's menu moves it to the top. The
  Flags menus list every flag, the built-in one included; the Flagged
  pills and the search filter take the built-in flag's name; and the
  rule editor lists the built-in flag with the others. Feed items keep
  the one flag they have, under its name and color.
