- Apple: **Folder menu behind the list title.** The folder name at the top
  of the message list is now a tappable affordance: on iPhone, iPad and
  Apple Vision Pro the title opens a menu, and on the Mac the name leads the
  list's action bar with a chevron. The menu lists subscribed folders, with
  the unsubscribed ones one tap further under "Other folders", the folder
  in view carrying the check mark wherever it sits. With nothing subscribed
  every folder is listed at the top level, as in the sidebar. A pick applies
  exactly as a sidebar tap does.
