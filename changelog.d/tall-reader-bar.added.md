- Apple: **Page buttons, Compose and a Message menu under a message in a
  tall window.** On an iPhone Duo open upright or folded like a laptop,
  and on iPad in portrait on iPadOS 27 and later, the bar under the
  message carries Reply, Archive or Delete, Flag, Read, Page Up, Page
  Down, Message and Compose. Page Up and Page Down move the message by
  most of a screen. The Message menu holds Load Images, the message's
  addresses with their actions (your own address first, its Revoke one
  level down), Move to Folder, Reader View and Plain Text, and View
  Source, View Headers and Print, so in laptop pose none of them needs
  a tap above the fold. Edit Draft's button is now a pencil, so it
  doesn't look like Compose.
