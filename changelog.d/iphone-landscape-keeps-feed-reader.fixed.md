- Apple: **Rotating an iPhone no longer drops the open feed item.** On the
  Plus / Max models, landscape reports a regular horizontal size class, and
  the signed-in root swapped its whole compact tab layout for the iPad split
  view on that change — discarding the Feeds tab, the item being read, and its
  scroll position, and landing on the mail INBOX; rotating back rebuilt the
  tabs at the launch landing. The layout is now chosen by device idiom first:
  an iPhone keeps the tab bar in every orientation, and only an iPad still
  switches between the split and the tabs on width changes.
