- Android: **One flags list.** The built-in flag is now an entry in
  Settings > Flags: first in the list, and named "Flagged" until you
  rename it. It can be renamed, recolored and moved like a flag you
  added; it cannot be switched off, and where those offer Delete flag it
  offers Reset to default. A flagged message shows a dot in that entry's
  color on its row and a named chip in the reader, in the same order as
  the list, in place of the star; search results show the dots too. A
  flagged feed item shows the same dot beside its date instead of a star
  at the row's edge.
