- Android: **One flags list.** The built-in flag is now an entry in
  Settings > Flags: first in the list, and named "Flagged" until you
  rename it. It can be renamed, recolored and moved like a flag you
  added; it cannot be switched off, and where those offer Delete flag it
  offers Reset to default. A flagged message shows a dot in that entry's
  color on its row and a named chip in the reader, in the same order as
  the list, in place of the star; search results show the dots too. A
  flagged feed item shows the same dot beside its date instead of a star
  at the row's edge. The first enabled flag in the list is the default
  flag, marked Default once there is a second one: the flag swipe, the
  star button in the reader, the selection bar's flag items, a search
  result's long-press and Ctrl+Shift+L set and clear it, and take its
  name and color once it is anything but the built-in flag under its
  original name. Make default in a flag's dialog moves it to the top.
  The flag menus on a message list every flag, the built-in one
  included; the Flagged pills and the search filter take the built-in
  flag's name; and the rule editor lists the built-in flag with the
  others. Feed items keep the one flag they have, under its name and
  color.
