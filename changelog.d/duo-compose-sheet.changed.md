- Apple: **Compose as a sheet on iPad and iPhone Duo.** New Message,
  Reply, Reply All, Forward, Edit Draft and mailto links now open a sheet
  over the window they came from on every iPhone, iPad and iPhone Duo,
  instead of a separate window on iPad and the open Duo. In a wide window
  the sheet sits on the trailing side; in book pose it keeps to the
  trailing page, and its discard question is an alert. It can no longer
  be swiped away, which used to drop the draft without asking: Cancel
  asks whether to keep it. While a reply is up, keyboard shortcuts and
  notification taps leave the message behind it alone, and a mailto
  waits for the reply to close. The Mac and visionOS still open a
  compose window; opening one on request from the sheet comes in a later
  change.
